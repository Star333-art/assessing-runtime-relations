# Artifact limitations

1. The complete experiments require public third-party telemetry that is not
   redistributed in this archive.
2. RCAEval RE2 is retained as a supporting boundary experiment. Its verified
   package path starts from frozen per-case component outputs; the historical
   raw Route-B graph builder is unavailable and is not represented as runnable.
3. MicroCERCL Ownership is adapter-derived from documented naming conventions,
   rather than supplied as a native observed relation.
4. The AIOps2022 result covers one retained cloudbed subset and is not a full
   replication of the core mechanism protocol.
5. Root time and native root layer are known during evaluation. Labels do not
   enter scoring or propagation, but the known layer restricts candidate
   ranking.
